# About: Thomas Aidan Curran

Thomas Aidan Curran is an Irish-American technologist and software entrepreneur. He held multiple c-suite executive
roles for Fortune 500 companies in the technology and media sector and he founded seven high-tech software companies
throughout his career. Currently, Thomas is advising private equity firms and technology companies in the area of
artificial intelligence (AI) cloud infrastructure, software development operations security, and digital product
transformation. He is also an angel investor in early-stage software companies focusing on AI empowerment for SMBs and
prosumer businesses.

### Corporate Leadership

Throughout his career, Thomas Aidan Curran has held multiple c-suite executive roles in Fortune 500 companies. He led
several transformation initiatives to migrate large-scale software systems to cloud-based infrastructure, including the
implementation of cloud-based software development operations and security practices.

#### Deutsche Boerse AG

At <Link target={'\_blank'} href={'https://www.deutsche-boerse.com/dbg-en/'}>Deutsche Boerse AG</Link>, the german
exchange for shares and securities, from 2015 to 2018, Thomas led Deutsche Boerse management's drive to adopt a new
multi-cloud infrastructure and create an innovative software company culture as the innovation and technology advisor.
This plan included deploying a modern, secure, cloud platform across the enterprise while starting a new agile product
development organization. During his tenure, he was also the Managing Director of <Link target={'\_blank'}
href={'https://www.deutsche-boerse.com/dbg-en/about-us/deutsche-boerse-group/db1-ventures'}>DB1 Ventures</Link>, the
corporate venture capital arm of Deutsche Boerse AG, where he was responsible for strategic investments in early-stage
software companies.

#### Hybris AG (acquired by SAP SE)

Thomas Aidan Curran joined <Link target={'\_blank'} href={'https://www.sap.com/products/crm/commerce-cloud.html'}>Hybris
AG</Link>, an e-commerce platform provider for enterprise brands, in 2013 as the CTO. He lead the transformation of the
on premise architecture to a cloud-based "commerce as a service" e-commerce platform. The successful transformation was
a prerequisite for the acquisition of Hybris AG by <Link target={'\_blank'} href={'https://www.sap.com/'}>SAP SE</Link>
and the integration into SAP Customer Experience Cloud, where it is available today as the SAP Commerce Cloud product.

#### Deutsche Telekom AG

At <Link target={'\_blank'} href={'https://www.telekom.com/en'}>Deutsche Telekom AG</Link>, the largest
telecommunications provider in Europe, Thomas served as the Chief Technology for Products and Innovation from 2009
to 2012. During this period, amongst others things, Thomas was responsible for the company's first cloud-based consumer
product, "Connected Life and Work", the development and rollout of the IPTV platform "T-Home Entertain" adn the
organizations shift to agile software development practices, titled "Software Factory". During his tenure he also served
as the Director of <Link target={'\_blank'} href={'https://laboratories.telekom.com/'}> T-Labs</Link>, the research and
development unit of Deutsche Telekom.

#### Bertelsmann AG

From 2001 to 2003, Thomas served as the CTO and CIO of <Link target={'\_blank'}
href={'https://www.bertelsmann.de/#st-1'}>Bertelsmann AG</Link>, a global media and services conglomerate. Together with
legendary CEO, <Link target={'\_blank'} href={'https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Middelhoff'}>Thomas
Middelhoff</Link>, he led the companies transformation from traditional to digital media. This included the acquisition
of <Link target={'\_blank'} href={'https://www.napster.com/'}>Napster</Link>, the first legal music download platform,
the attempted acquisition of <Link target={'\_blank'} href={'https://www.amazon.com/'}>Amazon</Link>, the, then, online
book store, and the business operations of <Link target={'\_blank'} href={'https://www.aol.com/'}>AOL Europe</Link>, the
online media and services portal. During this transformative period, Bertelsmann saw a doubling of its revenue.

### Investments & Venture Capital

Thomas Aidan Curran is an active angel investor in <Link href={'https://thomasaidancurran.com/portfolio'}>early-stage
software companies</Link>, focusing on AI empowerment for small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs) and prosumer

## Education

In 1983, Thomas graduated from the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania, with a degree in Statistics,
Decision Science, and Computer Science. Subsequently, he held teaching and research positions, including Director of
Computing at the Wharton School. He also served as a Scientific Advisor at the <Link target={'\_blank'}
href={'https://www.udk-berlin.de/en/'}>Berlin University of the Arts</Link>, as a Doctoral Researcher at the <Link
target={'\_blank'} href={'https://wzb.eu/en'}>Berlin Social Science Center</Link>, and a Research and Teaching Assistant
at <Link target={'\_blank'} href={'https://www.tu.berlin/en/'}>Technical University of Berlin</Link>.

## Pro Bono Work & Charity

Thomas Aidan Curran is a strong advocate for free and accessible education and has been involved in various pro bono
projects and charitable initiatives. He was involved in the Digital Media Project as a board member from 2003 to 2007,
helping to advance open standards for digital media rights management. From 2004 to 2008, he served as a Pro Bono
Advisor to the Chancellor of the <Link target={'\_blank'} href={'https://www.schools.nyc.gov/'}>New York City Department
of Education</Link> (formerly Board of Education). From 2013 to 2019, he supported <Link target={'\_blank'}
href={'https://en.serlo.org/'}>Serlo</Link>, an open E-Learning platform, as a Advisor and Donor.
